Over his 108 854 imported pages, a–z as a first keystroke went from 61.5–84.9 ms (median 66.7) to 1.0–48.2 ms (median 10.8); every one of the 36 letters and digits got faster, and `gi do ma co gh ne lo ap` went from 65–102 ms to 0.5–22 ms. It holds because the tiers are 10 000 apart and a score inside one spans under 1 000, so once the shoulder has handed back a page's worth at a tier the rows it left out were all a tier lower; below that the whole table is read, which is why a page whose only `z` is mid-word is still found.
